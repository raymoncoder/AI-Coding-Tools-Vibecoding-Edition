# 🛸 The Vibecoding Toolkit

![Vibecoding Banner](banner.png)

> **Vibecoding [verb]:** The art of manifesting complex software systems by maintaining a high-level creative flow, leveraging autonomous AI agents to handle the implementation details while you steer the "vibe" of the project.

Welcome to the curated treasury of tools designed for the **Agentic Era**. This isn't just a list of CLI tools; it's the infrastructure for the future of creation.

---

## 🌪️ Core Vibe Engines
*The heavy hitters that turn prompts into pull requests.*

- **[Claw Code](https://github.com/ultraworkers/claw-code)** — The apex predator of terminal agents. A high-performance Python/Rust reconstruction of the Claude Code architecture. Built for those who need instant repo awareness and surgical edits.
- **[Aider](https://github.com/Aider-AI/aider)** — The git-native pair programmer. It doesn't just write code; it manages your history, handles multi-file refactors, and communicates through the language of diffs.
- **[Open Interpreter](https://github.com/OpenInterpreter/open-interpreter)** — Your computer’s natural language layer. If you can think it, it can execute it—from data analysis to system configuration, directly in your shell.
- **[Cline](https://github.com/cline/cline)** — The autonomous architect. A model-agnostic powerhouse that plans, edits, and executes across your entire workspace, including the browser.
- **[Goose](https://github.com/block/goose)** — The extensible local companion. Designed to run on-device, Goose integrates seamlessly with MCP to become whatever tool you need it to be.
- **[gptme](https://github.com/gptme/gptme)** — Long-lived, self-modifying agents. With git-backed memory, these agents evolve alongside your codebase, remembering past decisions and learning your style.
- **[Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent)** — A self-improving beast from Nous Research. It builds its own skills, manages persistent memory, and reaches you wherever you are (Telegram, Slack, Discord).

## 🎨 Terminal Glam & TUIs
*Because vibecoding should look as good as it feels.*

- **[Crush](https://github.com/charmbracelet/crush)** — Glamorous agentic coding. A stunning TUI built with Go and Charmbracelet. It’s LSP-aware, multi-provider, and arguably the most beautiful way to watch an AI work.
- **[Warp](https://github.com/warpdotdev/Warp)** — The modern terminal with a soul. Its built-in AI mode isn't just a chatbot; it's an orchestrator that understands your terminal's state and suggests the next vibe-move.
- **[Devon](https://github.com/entropy-research/Devon)** — A TUI-first pair programmer that focuses on the "human-in-the-loop" experience. Autonomous planning with a clear, interactive interface.
- **[Smelt](https://github.com/leonardcser/smelt)** — The Vim-lovers' agent. A Rust-based TUI with four distinct modes (Normal, Plan, Apply, Yolo) and granular permissions for the safety-conscious vibecoder.

---

## 🚄 Parallel Engines & Orchestrators
*Scale your vibe by running entire swarms of agents.*

- **[vibe-kanban](https://github.com/BloopAI/vibe-kanban)** — The mission control for agents. A visual board to administer, track, and manage multiple autonomous coding sessions simultaneously.
- **[Loom](https://github.com/ghuntley/loom)** — The product evolution engine. It enables autonomous loops that allow products to "self-evolve" through multi-agent coordination.
- **[Parallel Code](https://github.com/johannesjo/parallel-code)** — Multi-threaded creation. Run Claude Code, Codex, and Gemini CLI in parallel git worktrees to tackle massive features in minutes.
- **[OpenCastle](https://github.com/monkilabs/opencastle)** — The agent workforce. It turns simple assistants into a coordinated team of 19 specialist agents, each handling a specific part of the SDLC.
- **[Aeon](https://github.com/aaronjmars/aeon)** — Unattended autonomy. Runs on GitHub Actions, orchestrating agents across 90+ skills on a schedule or reactive triggers.

## 🛠️ The Vibe Infrastructure
*The "glue" that keeps your agents safe, smart, and connected.*

- **[Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io)** — The universal language of tools. MCP allows you to connect your agents to any data source, API, or local service with zero friction.
- **[AgentLint](https://github.com/0xmariowu/AgentLint)** — Ensure your repo is agent-ready. Scans for structural clarity, instruction quality, and security to make sure your AI "gets" your code.
- **[brood-box](https://github.com/stacklok/brood-box)** — The safety net. A hardware-isolated microVM sandbox that lets your agents run wild without risking your host machine.
- **[Nex](https://github.com/nex-crm/nex-as-a-skill)** — Organizational memory. Connects your agents to email, Slack, and CRM to provide them with the ultimate context for what they're building.
- **[EchoCoding](https://github.com/launsion-boop/EchoCoding)** — The auditory vibe. Adds an audio layer to your coding sessions with hook-triggered soundscapes and voice interaction.
